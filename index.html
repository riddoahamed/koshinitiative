Kosh | Financial Literacy App & Workshops in Bangladesh

Kosh helps everyday Bangladeshis learn, save, invest, and grow through workshops, community programs, practical tools, and a gamified financial literacy app.

Financial literacy for Bangladesh

Kosh, also known as Kosh BD and Kosh Initiative, combines workshops, community programs, and a beta app so students, young professionals, and everyday Bangladeshis can understand how money works.

Kosh app and money check

The Kosh app includes a money readiness check, short explainers, calculators, comparisons, and learning paths built around Bangladesh-specific financial decisions.

Workshops and community programs

Kosh runs financial literacy sessions for schools, universities, teams, workplaces, businesses, and community organizations. Kosh is part of NSU Startups Next Cohort 4 as of May 2026.
